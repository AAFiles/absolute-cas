Using the calculator

Absolute CAS is an independent calculator for VCE maths. It is not affiliated with Texas Instruments. Your work stays in this browser. Close this guide with Esc or Close, and reopen it any time from “Using the calculator”.

Type with your keyboard

Click inside the calculator, then type. Your physical keyboard works straight away. Press Enter to calculate.

On-screen keys and touchpad

Select Show keypad to display the keys. The arrow pad moves the cursor, and the ● in the middle is the touchpad click. Each small label above a key is a second function: tap ctrl or shift on screen, then the key.

Shortcuts

  • Enter calculates the current entry.
  • Ctrl + S saves the document.
  • Ctrl + Z undoes your last change.
  • Tab moves to the next box inside a template.

Fractions and templates

Press ▯/▯ to insert a fraction template. Type the top, press Tab, then type the bottom. On the keypad, tap ctrl then ÷ for the a/b fraction label.

Clearing your work

  • Del deletes the character before the cursor.
  • Ctrl then Del clears the whole entry line.
  • doc, then File, then New Document starts a fresh document.
  • Clear session deletes every saved document and resets the calculator on this device.

Privacy

Calculations run in your browser, and documents are saved only in this browser. We don’t ask for your name, email or contact details, and the calculator doesn’t send what you type anywhere. On a shared device, use Clear session when you finish.

Exams

Internet devices aren’t allowed in VCAA exams. Use this calculator for study and practice, and check your exam rules.
